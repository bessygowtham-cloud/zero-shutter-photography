Ask on WhatsApp
How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, three to six months is comfortable — peak season dates go earlier. Maternity, baby and portrait sessions usually need two to three weeks' notice.

Do you travel outside the city?

Yes. We shoot across India and take destination weddings abroad. Travel and stay are quoted separately and transparently.

When is the right time for a maternity or newborn shoot?

Maternity sits best between 28 and 34 weeks. Newborn sessions are loveliest in the first two weeks, while babies still curl. Milestone shoots work at 6 months and again at one year.

How many photographs do we receive?

It depends on the event, but we deliver every frame worth keeping — hand-selected and individually graded, not a bulk export. A full wedding day typically runs several hundred finished images.

How long is the delivery?

A private preview within a week, the complete gallery within three weeks, and albums roughly four weeks after your final selections.

What does it cost?

Packages are built around your functions rather than sold off a shelf. Tell us your dates and requirements and we'll send a clear, itemised quote.
