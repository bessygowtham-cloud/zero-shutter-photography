The approach

A little direction.
Mostly, just you.

We give you enough direction to feel comfortable — where to stand, what to do with your hands — and then get out of the way. Most of what ends up in your gallery happens in the gaps between poses: the laugh, the glance, the moment you forget the camera is there.

Locations range from open fields and golden-hour light to a clean studio backdrop — whatever suits the portrait you're after.

Ask on WhatsApp
How far in advance should we book?

Two to three weeks is usually enough, though golden-hour outdoor slots in peak season fill faster — book earlier if the timing matters to you.

What should we wear?

We'll share a short styling guide once you book — generally, solid colours and soft textures photograph better than busy patterns.

Can we bring our kids or pets?

Of course — family portraits are one of our favourite sessions to shoot, chaos and all.

Outdoor or studio — what do you recommend?

Depends on the mood. Outdoor light gives a softer, more natural feel; studio gives a clean, controlled portrait. We can also do both in one session.

How long until delivery?

A private preview within a week and the full gallery within two weeks.
