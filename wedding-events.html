Ask on WhatsApp
How far in advance should we book?

Three to six months is comfortable. Peak season dates (November to February) go earlier, so it's worth locking your date as soon as the venue is confirmed.

Do you cover every function or just the wedding day?

Whatever you'd like covered — a single muhurtham or the full run from haldi to reception. Packages are built around your functions, not sold off a shelf.

Do you travel outside the city?

Yes. We shoot across India and take destination weddings abroad. Travel and stay are quoted separately and transparently.

How many photographs do we receive?

Every frame worth keeping — hand-selected and individually graded, not a bulk export. A full wedding day typically runs several hundred finished images.

How long until delivery?

A private preview within a week, the complete gallery within three weeks, and albums roughly four weeks after your final selections.
