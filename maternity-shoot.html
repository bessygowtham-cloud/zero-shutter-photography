When is the right time to shoot?

Maternity sits best between 28 and 34 weeks — the bump is well shaped and you're generally still comfortable for a relaxed session.

Can my partner or other children join?

Absolutely — many of our favourite frames include the whole family. Let us know who's joining when you book so we can plan for it.

Studio or outdoors — which is better?

Both work well. Studio gives us full control over light and backdrop; outdoors brings in natural texture and golden-hour tones. We'll help you pick based on the mood you want.

How long does a session take?

Usually 60–90 minutes, with breaks whenever you need them. There's no fixed shot list to rush through.

How long until delivery?

A private preview within a week and the complete, colour-graded gallery within two to three weeks.
